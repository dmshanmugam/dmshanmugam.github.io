Hi! My name is Divya. I am a National Library of Medicine Fellow at Columbia, based at the Department of Biomedical Informatics.

My research focuses on how we can effectively embed AI systems within human systems, with a particular focus on healthcare. I often think about how the data we use to train, evaluate, and update our models governs the reliability of a machine learning system, and how we can improve systems by altering the data we use. My recent work develops methods to audit the data used to train AI systems ([NWH 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.04133); [JAMA HF 2026](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamahealthforum.2026.0491)); to evaluate systems when labels are limited or unreliable ([CHI 2023](https://doi.org/10.1145/3544548.3581482); [NeurIPS 2025](/pdfs/Neurips_2025_SSME_final.pdf)); and to improve the reliability of pretrained systems at test time ([ICCV 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.11156); [CVPR 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22764)).

I am always excited to work with students and collaborators on problems where real clinical settings raise new technical questions. Previously, I was a postdoc at Cornell Tech, advised by Emma Pierson and Jenna Wiens. Before that, I earned my Ph.D. at MIT, where I was lucky to work with John Guttag.
